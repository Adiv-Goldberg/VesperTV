## VesperTV 1.0.52 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 555

### Media Hub
- **New look:** the title you're on fills the top of the page with its logo art, a "Movie • Genre • Year • runtime" line, its IMDb rating and four lines of description, over a bigger backdrop.
- **Wide focused card:** the card you're on turns into a wide picture with the title's logo; the others stay posters.
- **New left panel:** the same glass look as the top bar. It folds into a slim strip of icons while you browse the rows, and opens again when you press Left.
- **Search moved to the left panel:** press OK on Search and pick Movies or TV Shows from the small menu that slides out. Phones keep Search in the top bar.
- **Expanded catalog** shows the focused title's backdrop and details at the top.

### Sports Trak
- **Rugby:** Six Nations, Premiership, URC, Top 14, Champions Cup, Super Rugby Pacific, Rugby World Cup, Test matches and NRL, with scores and tables.
- **Cricket:** IPL, Big Bash, PSL, SA20, CPL and T20 Blast, with fixtures and runs (no tables).
- **PDC darts:** one card per tournament day (no individual matches or scores).
- Add them in League manager under Rugby, Cricket and Darts.
- **Team ranks:** team names show their table position, like Blackhawks (15); college teams show their poll ranking.
- **Watch game button** (off by default, Settings > Sports Trak): when a game you set a reminder for starts, a Watch game button appears in the player. Up from the player bar, then OK, switches to the game.
- **Goals alert for a whole league:** hold OK on a game, or on a league in League manager.
- **Goal alerts show who scored** when the sports feed has it.
- **Better channel finding for fights**, including channels that only name one fighter (Canelo).
- **Sports Mode rows** scroll only as far as needed and no longer slide under the side panels.
- **Fixed:** on days when the clocks change, tomorrow's games were missing and the day strip's "tomorrow" showed today.

### Player
- **Up Next setting** (Settings > Playback > Up Next): start the next episode 30 seconds before the end, when the episode ends, or never.
- Hold Right to resume no longer also jumps to the last channel.

### Google TV / Chromecast
- The D-pad can leave a text field after typing (the on-screen keyboard used to trap it).

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45**. This release contains an Android APK only; Windows has not been rebuilt or tested for these changes. Its existing download remains available on the website and the 1.0.45 release.

### Install
Update through Settings > Check for Updates, download app-release.apk, or use Downloader code **1760256**. Install over your existing app to keep your setup.
