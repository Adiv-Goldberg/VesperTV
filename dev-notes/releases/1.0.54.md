## VesperTV 1.0.54 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 579

### Playlist editor (Test flight)
A new way to clean up your playlists from your phone or PC: **Settings > Playlists & guide > Playlist editor (Test flight)**. Scan the QR code with your phone, or open the address on your PC and type the code shown on the TV.
- Rename channels, change logos, hide channels in bulk, and add rules that keep working after every update (for example remove `US| ` from every name, or hide everything with `PPV`).
- **Hide lower-quality copies**: keep only the best copy of each channel; the others become automatic backups.
- **Groups**: rename, merge, reorder and hide groups.
- **Fallback channels**: choose which channels play, in your order, when a channel fails.
- **Several playlists**: use your other playlists as backups, or combine them into one lineup.
- **Stream test** (optional, with a warning): reads each stream's real quality and finds dead links. Some providers block accounts that open many streams in a row, so test small batches.
- A **How to** tab explains everything. Your provider's playlist is never changed, and every change can be undone.

This is a **Test flight**: it is new, so please report anything odd with Settings > Send log.

### Playback
- **Automatic backup copy**: when a live channel fails to start or keeps dropping, the app switches to another copy of it (FHD, then HD...) or the same channel in your other playlists, without an error screen (switch on the Playlist editor screen).
- **Live buffer** (Settings > Playback > Live buffer): store a few seconds before playing so short server dropouts don't show. Bigger hides longer dropouts, but channels start later.
- Fewer requests to a provider that refuses your account, and groups still show when the provider's category list can't be loaded.

### Accessibility
- Much better with the phone's largest text and display size: screens scroll and buttons wrap instead of being cut off.
- TalkBack reads button names, guide programmes with their times, opens hold menus with double-tap-and-hold, and says which channel starts playing.

### Phones
- Media Hub: one **Search** button and a **Menu** with everything else; Movies / TV Shows is a dropdown; the first tap on a poster shows it, the second opens it.
- Show pages: seasons are a dropdown.
- Home, title pages and Multiview no longer sit under the status bar.
- The player's lock icon fades away and comes back when you touch the screen.
- Moving a channel or group (hold, Move Up / Down) shows big Up / Down / Done buttons.

### Media Hub
- The title's picture now fills the screen behind the rows.
- A show with hundreds of episodes no longer freezes the app when it opens.
- **Show under Other** is now off for every source; turn it on in Settings > Media Hub sources for sources you want kept apart.

### Live TV & Sports Trak
- New opt-in **Recently added** group (Settings > Tabs): channels your playlists added in the last 14 days.
- New leagues: Chilean Primera División, Copa Chile, Chilean Supercopa, Liga Mexicana de Béisbol, Liga Mexicana del Pacífico and LNBP (basketball).
- TV: hold OK on a channel > Rename.
- Show pages: moving left through many seasons scrolls back again.
- Google TV: the keyboard lets go of the remote after you leave a text field.

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45** (its download was refreshed with a fix for live channels staying black with some providers). This release contains an Android APK only.

### Install
Update through Settings > Check for Updates, download app-release.apk, or use Downloader code **1760256**. Install over your existing app to keep your setup.
