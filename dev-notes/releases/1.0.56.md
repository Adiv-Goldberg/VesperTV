## VesperTV 1.0.56 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 606

### Faster with big playlists
- Moving around the Live TV guide, the channel list and the Catch-up group is much quicker when you have many playlists or a very large one. The app no longer goes through every channel on each press of the remote.
- Movies and TV Shows rows load faster from the app's catalog when you have several playlists.
- Fewer slowdowns at startup on TV boxes with large catalogs.
- The first launch after this update may take a little longer while the app reorganises its movie and show catalog. It happens once.

### Playlist servers that are down or busy
- If a playlist's server doesn't answer, or says it's receiving too many requests, the app tries once more and then pauses that playlist's movie and show loading for two minutes, instead of asking again and again. Rows you already have keep showing, and loading resumes as soon as the server answers.

### Send log
- Logs now include more precise timings, so slowness reports can be diagnosed faster.

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45**. This release contains an Android APK only.

### Install
Update through Settings > Check for Updates, download app-release.apk, or use Downloader code **1760256**. Install over your existing app to keep your setup.
