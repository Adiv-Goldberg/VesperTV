## VesperTV 1.0.51 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 541

### New
- **German:** Deutsch is now in Settings > General > Language, and is picked automatically on German devices.
- **Expanded catalog:** a new look for the full poster grid. The focused title, year and rating show at the top over its backdrop, with the grid below. Turn on Settings > Interface > Tabs > Open groups as expanded catalog to open Movies and TV Shows groups straight into it.
- **Support and Discord:** Settings has a Support VesperTV entry (a coffee or a Supporter membership; the app stays free, no ads, same app for everyone) and, under Device & data, a Discord entry with a QR code for direct support.

### Live TV guide
- **New Guide settings page** under Settings > Interface. The Live TV guide options moved there from Theme.
- **Compact guide:** shorter Timeline rows, so more channels fit in the same space.
- **Custom EPG URL:** Settings > EPG > General lets you pick a playlist and paste your own XMLTV link for it.

### Sports Trak
- **Sports Mode:** Right on a channel in the Live TV guide closes the guide and moves into the games. In the game feed, OK plays the game, and OK again opens it full screen (Right no longer jumps to full screen).
- **Transparent popups:** goal alerts and reminders can drop their background and outline, leaving only the logo and text. Settings > Interface > Goals alert, with a Show a test popup button to preview it.

### Settings and startup
- **Fast startup is now on by default.** The app opens without waiting for Movies and TV Shows to load; they fill in a few seconds later. Your own choice is kept if you changed it. Settings > General > Startup.
- **Resume reminder pill is off by default.** Turn it on under Settings > Playback > Resume reminder.
- **Media Hub playback** (largest-file option) moved to the Media Hub category.
- Switch rows in Settings have proper spacing inside their outline.

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45**. This release contains an Android APK only; Windows has not been rebuilt or tested for these changes. Its existing download remains available on the website and the 1.0.45 release.

### Install
Update through Settings > Check for Updates, download app-release.apk, or use Downloader code **1760256**. Install over your existing app to keep your setup.
